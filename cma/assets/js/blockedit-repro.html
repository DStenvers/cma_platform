Steps

2. — a normal collect_htmls(); the field keeps its content.
3. — runs the record-switch → save sequence: empties the field (models clearForm's setData('')), removes the blocks (blockedit_clear()), then saves (collect_htmls()) before the blocks are rebuilt.
— restore the seeded value and reload the editor.
